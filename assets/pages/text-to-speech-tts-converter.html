Paste any text, even a full chapter, and listen to it read aloud. Choose the voice and speed, and pause any time.

How to listen to text

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Pick a voice and speed.
  3. Click Read aloud. Pause, resume or stop whenever you like.

Questions

Is it free?

Completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you use it. Light ads keep the site running.

Why are the voices different on my phone?

The voices come from your device. Phones and computers each have their own set.

Can I download the audio?

Browsers don’t allow saving these voices as a file, but you can listen as often as you like.
