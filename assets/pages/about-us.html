GroupChatOn is a small fan-run website where readers enjoy carefully proofread English chapters of Korean web novels, free and in a comfortable reading layout.

What we do

We take existing fan translations, proofread them line by line for grammar, names and flow, and publish them in reading order with easy chapter navigation. Our current series:

  • Trash of the Count’s Family (Part I and Part II)
  • The Reincarnated Assassin is a Genius Swordsman
  • Entomologist In Sichuan Tang Clan
  • Chronicles of the Heavenly Demon

Who we are

GroupChatOn is run by Mayank, a reader who started proofreading favourite series so more people could enjoy them, together with the translators credited at the end of every chapter.

Made for reading

  • Light, sepia, dark and black themes, with adjustable font, size and spacing.
  • Your place is remembered, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Ads are kept light and placed so they never interrupt a scene.

Free tools

We also offer free online tools for PDFs, images, text and links. They run in your browser, so your files stay private.

Respecting the authors

Every novel belongs to its original author and publisher. If you can, please support the official release. Rights holders can reach us through the contact page, and we respond quickly. See our disclaimer.
