Pull out the pages you need, save every page as its own PDF, or split a long PDF into equal parts. Nothing is uploaded.

How to split a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Pick Extract pages, Every page separately or Split every N pages.
  3. For extracting, type pages like 1-3, 5, 8-.
  4. Click Split PDF and download your files (or all as one ZIP).

Questions

Is it private?

Yes. Your files are processed inside your own browser and are never uploaded to our server or anyone else’s. Close the page and they are gone.

Is it free?

Completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you use it. Light ads keep the site running.

What does “8-” mean?

From page 8 to the last page.

Will the quality drop?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are, with text, images and links intact.
