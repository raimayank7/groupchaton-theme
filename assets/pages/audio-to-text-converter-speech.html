Speak and watch your words appear as text, in English, Hindi, Punjabi and many more languages. Edit, copy or download the result.

How to type with your voice

  1. Pick the language you will speak.
  2. Click Start speaking and allow the microphone.
  3. Speak naturally. Say “comma” or “full stop” for punctuation in some languages.
  4. Click Stop, then copy or download your text.

Questions

Which browsers work?

Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Safari. Firefox does not support speech typing yet.

Is my voice recorded?

The browser sends your speech to its own speech service to turn it into text. GroupChatOn never receives or stores it.

Is it free?

Completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you use it. Light ads keep the site running.
