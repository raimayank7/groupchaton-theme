Join several PDF files into one, in exactly the order you want. Free, fast and private: your files never leave your device.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Click Choose PDF files or drag them into the box.
  2. Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to put them in order.
  3. Click Merge PDFs.
  4. Download your merged PDF.

Good to know

  • Pages keep their original size, text and links.
  • Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first.
  • Need only some pages? Use Split PDF first.

Questions

Is it private?

Yes. Your files are processed inside your own browser and are never uploaded to our server or anyone else’s. Close the page and they are gone.

Is it free?

Completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you use it. Light ads keep the site running.

Is there a size limit?

Only your device’s memory. Most phones handle a few hundred pages easily.

Can I change the order after adding files?

Yes. Move files up or down, or remove one with ✕, before you merge.
