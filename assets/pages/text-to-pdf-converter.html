Type or paste text, or open a .txt file, and save it as a clean PDF with your choice of font, size and paper.

How to make a PDF from text

  1. Type or paste your text (it is kept on this device while you work).
  2. Add an optional title and pick the font and size.
  3. Click Make PDF and download it.

Questions

Is it private?

Yes. Your files are processed inside your own browser and are never uploaded to our server or anyone else’s. Close the page and they are gone.

Is it free?

Completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you use it. Light ads keep the site running.

Does it work for Hindi or Korean?

The built-in PDF fonts cover Latin letters only. For other scripts, use HTML to PDF with “Print / Save as PDF”.
