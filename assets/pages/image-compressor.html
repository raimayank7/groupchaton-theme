Make photos much smaller for WhatsApp, email, forms and websites: resize them and lower the file size without a visible difference.

How to compress images

  1. Choose your photos (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  2. Pick the longest side, e.g. 1920 px, and the format.
  3. Leave quality around 75% and click Compress.
  4. Compare the before and after sizes and download.

Tips

  • Forms that ask for “under 100 KB”: choose 800 px and quality 60–70%.
  • WebP is usually the smallest; JPG works everywhere.

Questions

Is it private?

Yes. Your files are processed inside your own browser and are never uploaded to our server or anyone else’s. Close the page and they are gone.

Is it free?

Completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you use it. Light ads keep the site running.

Will my photo look worse?

At 70–80% quality most people cannot see a difference, while the file is often 60–90% smaller.
