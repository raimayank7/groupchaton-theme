Pack files or a whole folder into one ZIP, or open a ZIP and save the files inside, right in your browser.

How to make or open a ZIP

  1. Pick Make a ZIP or Open a ZIP.
  2. Choose your files, folder or ZIP file.
  3. Click Create ZIP, or download the files you need from inside the ZIP.

Questions

Is it private?

Yes. Your files are processed inside your own browser and are never uploaded to our server or anyone else’s. Close the page and they are gone.

Is it free?

Completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you use it. Light ads keep the site running.

Will zipping make photos and videos smaller?

Barely: they are already compressed. ZIP shrinks text, documents and spreadsheets the most. For photos, use the Image Compressor.

Can it open password-protected ZIPs?

No, not yet.
