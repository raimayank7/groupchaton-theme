Start a WhatsApp chat with any number without adding it to your contacts. Great for delivery people, shops and one-time chats.

How to message without saving the number

  1. Pick the country.
  2. Type the phone number.
  3. Add a message if you like.
  4. Tap Open in WhatsApp, or copy the link to share it.

Questions

Is it free?

Completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you use it. Light ads keep the site running.

Does the other person see anything different?

No. It is a normal WhatsApp chat.

Is this an official WhatsApp feature?

Yes. It uses WhatsApp’s own wa.me links. We are not connected to WhatsApp or Meta.
