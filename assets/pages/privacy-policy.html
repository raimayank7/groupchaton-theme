This policy explains what information GroupChatOn (www.groupchaton.com) collects when you visit, how it is used and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you use the contact form, “Report a mistake” or “Request a novel”, you send us your name (optional), your email address and your message. We use them only to reply and to fix or plan chapters, and we never sell or share them.

Information collected automatically

  • Google Analytics counts visits and pages read (approximate location, device and browser) so we know what readers enjoy. It uses cookies and does not tell us who you are.
  • Google AdSense shows ads. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info. Read how Google uses data from partner sites.
  • Blogger (Google) hosts this site and keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses.
  • Disqus runs the comments. It loads only when you tap “Show comments”, and its own privacy policy applies.

Stored on your own device

To make reading comfortable, your browser saves your reading settings (theme, font, size), the chapters you read and where you stopped, and, if you choose, your name and email for the feedback form. This stays in your browser’s local storage, is never sent to us, and is removed when you clear your browser data.

Free tools

Our tools (PDF, image, text and QR tools) work inside your browser. Your files are not uploaded to us. Three tools use outside services: the URL shortener sends the link to TinyURL, the speech-to-text tool uses your browser’s speech service, and the OCR tool downloads its language data from a public library server.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. You can block or delete them in your browser settings; the site will still work, but ads may become less relevant.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, through the contact page. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights, and we will honour them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
