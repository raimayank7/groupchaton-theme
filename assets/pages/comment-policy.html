Comments are where readers share theories, favourite scenes and reactions. A few simple rules keep them fun for everyone.

✅ Be kindDisagree with ideas, not people. No insults, hate or harassment.
🙈 Mark spoilersStart with “Spoiler:” when you talk about later chapters, so others can skip it.
🚫 No spamNo ads, referral links, self-promotion or copy-pasted / AI-written filler.
🔒 Stay privateDon’t share phone numbers, addresses or anyone’s personal details.
🎯 Stay on topicKeep comments about the chapter or the series.
⚖️ Keep it legalNo explicit, illegal or harmful content, and no links to pirated copies.

Typos and requests

Found a mistake? Use Report a mistake: it reaches us faster than a comment. Want a new series? Request a novel.

Moderation

We may remove comments that break these rules and block accounts that keep doing so. Comments in English are preferred so everyone can join in.
