See every page of your PDF, then rotate sideways scans, delete the pages you don’t need and move pages into the right order.

How to organize a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF. Thumbnails of every page appear.
  2. Use ↻ to rotate, 🗑 to delete and ← → to move a page.
  3. Click Save PDF and download it.

Questions

Is it private?

Yes. Your files are processed inside your own browser and are never uploaded to our server or anyone else’s. Close the page and they are gone.

Is it free?

Completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you use it. Light ads keep the site running.

Can I undo a delete?

Yes. Deleted pages stay faded until you save: tap ↺ on the page, or Restore deleted.

How many pages can it handle?

Up to 300 pages at a time. Split bigger files first.
